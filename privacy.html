Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: August 10, 2026
Company: EVOSMART VIA LLC | Domain: evosmart-via.com

This Privacy Policy describes how EVOSMART VIA LLC ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal information when you access or use our website evosmart-via.com and our mobile software applications, including ParentScout, distributed via platforms including Google Play.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that identifies, relates to, or describes you ("Personal Information"). Depending on your interaction with our mobile applications and website, we may collect:

  • Account & Contact Data: Email address, name, phone number, and optional profile data provided upon registration or support inquiries.
  • Device & Usage Data: IP address, mobile device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and crash diagnostic logs required for app stability and performance.
  • Application Interaction Data: Feature usage analytics and preferences within ParentScout and our digital software solutions.

2. How We Use Your Information

EVOSMART VIA LLC utilizes your personal information for legitimate business purposes including:

  • Providing, operating, and maintaining ParentScout and our mobile software services.
  • Verifying user accounts and enforcing platform security.
  • Responding to customer support requests and technical inquiries.
  • Analyzing application performance, debugging crash logs, and optimizing user experience.
  • Complying with legal obligations, regulatory mandates, and platform guidelines (such as Google Play Developer Policies).

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS/SSL in transit, AES-256 at rest) and strict organizational access controls to safeguard your data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. However, no data transmission over the Internet or electronic storage can be guaranteed 100% secure.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

EVOSMART VIA LLC does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share data with trusted service providers under non-disclosure obligations to perform core app functionality (such as cloud hosting, crash reporting, or security monitoring), or when required by US court order or federal law.

5. User Data Rights & Erasure

Users of our mobile applications have the right to request access, correction, or permanent deletion of their personal information. To request data account deletion or exercise your privacy rights under California CCPA/CPRA, please contact us directly at info@evosmart-via.com.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services and mobile applications are intended for general adult audiences and families. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or under 16 in applicable jurisdictions) without verifiable parental consent.
