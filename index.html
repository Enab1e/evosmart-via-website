Our Mobile Application

ParentScout

Discover places where it is genuinely comfortable to go with children.

The Information Parents Actually Need

Standard map applications show where a restaurant is located and its general review score. But parents need completely different details: Is there a kids' menu? High chairs? A baby changing station? An indoor play room? Is it easy to enter with a stroller, and is the staff welcoming to families with toddlers?

ParentScout gathers all this essential information into a single intuitive mobile platform.

🗺️

Smart Interactive Filters

Instantly locate nearby venues on an interactive map filtered by specific family parameters:

  • Kids' menu & nutrition options
  • High chairs & seating boosters
  • Baby changing tables & nursery rooms
  • Stroller accessibility & ramps
  • Outdoor & indoor play areas
  • Children's activities & events
🤖

AI Parent Insights

A high restaurant rating doesn't guarantee a smooth visit with a 2-year-old. ParentScout's AI analyzes visitor reviews to highlight key family details — staff attitude toward kids, stroller space, noise level, and amenities — saving you from reading hundreds of reviews.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Parent-Verified Data

Venue information is continuously verified and updated by a community of parents. Families help confirm which amenities are truly present, building an accurate, real-time map created by parents for parents.

Find the Perfect Place in Seconds

Instead of multiple Google searches, scrolling through photo galleries, and scanning dozens of reviews — perform one quick search in ParentScout:

"Restaurant near me with a kids' menu, high chairs, and stroller space."

"Cafe with a dedicated play area suitable for toddlers."

"Venue with an indoor play area for a rainy day."

Can we go there with kids?

ParentScout is built so that answering this question takes only a few seconds. Less time searching, more time together.
